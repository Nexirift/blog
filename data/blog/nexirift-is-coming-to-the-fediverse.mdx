Last month, I announced the discontinuation of Nexirift. This was a hard decision to make and not one that I wanted to make.

Today, I decided that Nexirift will continue. However, at least for the time being, we will be moving to the Fediverse.

We are unsure what software we will be using. When the platform goes live, you will be able to view the source code, likely a fork of Misskey.

For the time being, unlike last time, we do not plan to register as a full company, and we will not be acting for-profit.

The current plan I have for the project does not require any funding, so I do not believe that we will be taking donations.

I want to note that any promised features are unlikely to be implemented due to the lack of funding and the pre-built software.

I can confirm that the following features are very likely to be implemented:

- The ability to interact with users from:
  - The Fediverse: **Both** ways - Software like Mastodon, Pleroma, etc.
  - Twitter: Only on **Nexirift** - Twitter users cannot see your profile.
  - Bluesky: **Both** ways - There seems to be no limitations.
- Custom Emojis (This is server-controlled, not user-controlled).
- Reactions (Instead of liking, use an emoji - it will default to a heart).
- Custom themes and sounds.
- Export all your data to another instance.
- Posts can be scheduled.
- Privacy features like locked accounts.
- Avatar decorations (we likely won't be animating them).
- Verified artist's will likely get increased file size limits.
  - Our current [Verified Artist](/blog/introducing-artist-programs)'s requirements does apply to this.
- And a lot more...

I just want to note that most of these features are implemented because of the server software and are not Nexirift-specific.

All moderators will be 18+ and will be thoroughly verified to ensure that they will protect and serve the community.

Our commitment to transparency is still strong, and we plan to be open about any changes to the platform and any reports filed.

For those who do not wish to use Nexirift, you can still interact with the userbase on other instances of the Fediverse.

Our planned release date is prior to January 2026. We plan to open with registrations that require approval. <small>and maybe allowing users to invite others to join without needing moderation approval.</small>
