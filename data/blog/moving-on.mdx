As of the date of writing this post, I have decided that it is best to move on from this project. Let me explain why.

Ever since I was young, I have always wanted to make a social media platform. I wanted to make a difference in this world in some way. I started half a decade ago and made multiple prototypes.

There are multiple factors that come into play when making this decision. One of the biggest ones is the various problems with the world right now, including privacy invading age verification laws.

These laws would put a big strain on the development of Nexirift and would essentially kill the project in the affected areas, including the country where Nexirift is developed, Australia.

Another reason is the misinformation from a previous project that Nexirift was developed from. That project should have never been released in the state it was and I was not expecting it to blow up in users.

The previously semi-successful platform suffered massive problems, none of which resulted in any leaks. It was poorly managed, but the server hosting it had standard security practices.

The misinformation mentioned has been around for a while but never in a capacity that made me worry. It was not until a recent event that I realised that it might be pointless to continue.

There is one last factor and that is the fact that some people do not believe in the project. There are people who are rightfully skeptical that the project will be able to survive.

Maybe, one day I will come back to this project, but for now, I think it is time to move on. Thank you to those who have supported it along the way.
