We are excited to announce various community programs for artists on our platform. These programs are designed to help artists stand out and grow on our platform.

## Verified Artists Program

The verified artists program is a way for users to identify official artists on the platform. This is intended to help prevent users from being scammed by fake artists. All of our programs have requirements for users to meet in order to be eligible.

### Requirements [^1]

_Don't meet these requirements? [Apply for the growing artists program](#growing-artists-program)._

- Must have a certain amount of followers on an applicable platform [^2]
  - Nexirift: 100
  - Twitter: 1,000
  - Bluesky: 1,000
  - Instagram: 1,000
- Original artwork validation
- Required proof of creation (timelapses, WIP shots, etc.)
- No AI-generated artwork is permitted in this program.

[^1]: If you are joining this program via the Growing Artists Program, certain requirements, such as the follower count will be frozen for you.

[^2]: TikTok and YouTube due to the short videos gaining more traction. Follower counts are subject to change, existing verified artists will not be affected.

## Growing Artists Program

The growing artists program is available to all users on our platform. It is designed to help users wishing to join the verified artists program be able to reach the requirements necessary to be eligible.

### Requirements

- None!

### Features

- Detailed tips on how to grow your reach
- Access to help from existing verified artists [^7]
- ... and more!

[^7]: This does not include all verified artists, only those who have partnered with us internally.
